The brief contains a roadmap that:
- Starts with a proof of concept (inc-0 / PoC) that runs end to end (add an expense and see it listed or totalled) with one command, and says it has a smoke check rather than a unit-test suite.
- Follows with 1 to 3 further increments, each with what it delivers, the tests it adds, and a demo command.
- Has numbered or labelled acceptance criteria that the increments reference.
- Lists assumptions or decisions taken, and a test strategy consistent with: smoke check for the PoC, tests with each increment, broader QA at the end.
